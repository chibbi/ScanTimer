How to time your scans: You can either use the microscope movement and count 5–10 movements, since each movement has a clear sound, or count the scanning positions on the OPUS display. Press Start Timing when the first scanning position has finished, then press Stop Timing when the chosen later position has finished. Enter the number of intervals measured. Timing at least 5 intervals is recommended. This stopwatch runs separately from the Start now button above.
